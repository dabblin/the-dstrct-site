Act I • Historical Lineage

ROOTED IN LEGACY. BUILT FOR THE FUTURE.

01

Jack Johnson & The Cotton Club Genesis

In the 1920s, heavyweight champion Jack Johnson opened the Club Deluxe in Harlem, which gangster Owney Madden later acquired and transformed into the legendary Cotton Club. That era forged the unbreakable bond between championship boxing, Black culture, high-society glamour, and cinematic entertainment.

02

The Cinematic “Pygmalion” Vision

Drawing direct inspiration from the upcoming cinematic production Pygmalion at The Cotton Club, Onie Rivers and THE DSTRCT weave vintage silent-film drama with contemporary high-voltage live sports.

03

Breaking Barriers: Phat Pink Laces

Decades of producing live arena tours, national music galas, and boxing awards shows culminate in PHAT PINK LACES. A women-led powerhouse redefining the commercial authority and artistic grandeur of world-class boxing.
